About the project

Clinical reasoning support, built with medical education in mind.

Davidson AI is an early-stage health technology project developing a prototype/MVP for AI-assisted clinical decision support and structured medical learning.

Mission

To make high-quality medical reasoning easier to access, understand, and verify by combining structured knowledge, retrieval-augmented generation, and transparent citations.

Vision

A future where physicians, students, and health teams can use responsible AI tools that explain clinical logic clearly while preserving source traceability and professional judgment.

Problem

Medical knowledge is abundant, but hard to use at the point of reasoning.

Information overload

Clinicians and students must navigate dense references, guidelines, notes, and question banks under time pressure.

Weak traceability

AI-generated answers can be useful, but they need transparent links back to trusted medical source material.

Reasoning gaps

Medical learners often need step-by-step clinical logic, not just short factual answers.

Our solution

A structured AI layer for evidence-aware clinical reasoning.

Davidson AI combines retrieval, structured medical metadata, and large language models to support clinical explanations, education workflows, and citation-backed answers.

Development roadmap

From prototype to validated clinical education platform.

  1. Phase 1Knowledge base and RAG prototype

    Prepare structured medical content, retrieval experiments, and citation workflows.

  2. Phase 2MVP interface

    Build a usable question-answering and clinical reasoning interface for controlled testing.

  3. Phase 3Evaluation and safety review

    Assess retrieval quality, hallucination resistance, clinical relevance, and educational usefulness.

  4. Phase 4Pilot collaboration

    Explore academic, clinician, and infrastructure partnerships for further validation.

Why cloud infrastructure

Medical AI needs dependable compute, storage, and deployment foundations.

Davidson AI requires infrastructure that can support document processing, vector search, model integration, secure prototyping, and future scaling as the product matures.

  • Scalable compute for AI-assisted workflows
  • Reliable storage for structured medical knowledge
  • Deployment paths for MVP testing and collaboration
  • Performance foundations for future clinical education use cases
